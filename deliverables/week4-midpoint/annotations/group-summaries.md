## Annotations

### S1. Generic feedback was tried and found useless

> "Generic AI feedback did not offer us much" (English summary, paragraph 1)

**Labels:** useful / keep
**Why:** The group states it about its own experience rather than about
interviewees, which makes it first-hand and consistent with what the report says
interviewees reported, and two independent sources agreeing is rare in this
material.

### S2. Simulated interviewees pointed the opposite way to real ones

> "while five real interviewees were negative" (English summary, paragraph 2)

**Labels:** surprising / keep
**Why:** A reversal rather than a degradation is the strongest single result in
the Week 3 material, and it is the reason the proposal treats a generated item
as a proposal rather than as data.

### S3. The group caught the report flattening one voice into many

> "sound like a group conclusion" (English summary, paragraph 3)

**Labels:** useful / keep
**Why:** The group audited a tool's output against what it remembered of the
room and found a specific failure, which is both a finding about AI synthesis
and a constraint on how the Week 4 summary may cite that report.

### S4. Record and suggestion must stay distinct

> "the overview represents what we said" (English summary, paragraph 4)

**Labels:** useful / keep
**Why:** This is the product's central rule in the group's own words, arrived at
from the FrameIt conversation rather than from the build, and it is the single
best line to put on the value slide.

### S5. The automatic or on-request question is still open

> "we have not settled whether suggestions should appear" (English summary, paragraph 4)

**Labels:** unclear / keep
**Why:** The group names two undecided questions, when suggestions appear and
what happens when general advice contradicts an earlier project decision, and
keeping them open and visible is more useful to the proposal than an invented
answer would be.

### S6. Visible AI errors are the safe kind

> "Zichtbare AI-fouten breken vertrouwen sneller dan onzichtbare"
> (Dutch summary, finding 5)

**Labels:** surprising / keep
**Why:** A distorted render is dismissed at a glance while a plausible wrong
number is not, which inverts the usual assumption and argues that the danger in
our own tool is a reading that looks right, so the visible provisional marking
is protection against the harder case and not decoration.

### S7. Partial delegation, not full delegation

> "Bouw voor gedeeltelijke delegatie, niet volledige delegatie"
> (Dutch summary, what we take forward)

**Labels:** useful / keep
**Why:** It gives the proposal a defensible line on where the tool stops, people
keep the creative and high-skill steps, and it is consistent with the report's
observation about sketching without resting on that one voice.

### S8. Eight findings, three sources, no counts

> "3 interviewtranscripten uit deze map" (Dutch summary, header)

**Labels:** unclear / question
**Why:** The summary names its sources honestly but no finding says how many
people it came from, so a reader cannot tell a finding that three interviewees
shared from one that a single person said once.

### S9. Five interviewees, or three, is ambiguous

> "while five real interviewees were negative" (English summary, paragraph 2)

**Labels:** unclear / question
**Why:** The folder's manifest lists three recordings while this passage speaks
of five real interviewees, and the passage supports two readings: either the
five were interviewed by somebody the group interviewed, as part of that
person's own project, or the group's Week 3 interviews were more numerous than
the recordings kept, so the group must say which before the number goes on a
slide.

### S10. The 3D modelling thread has no artefact in this folder

> "Uit het Blender-gesprek komt een expliciete spanning"
> (Dutch summary, finding 8)

**Labels:** unconvincing / reject
**Why:** Reject for this deliverable, not as an observation: the conversation it
rests on is not in the Week 3 folder, so the finding cannot be traced to an
artefact in the submission, and it is about learning a tool alone rather than
about a meeting, which is not the scenario the proposal narrowed to. `GAPS.md`
records the missing file.

### S11. What the group says it takes forward is already built

> "trace an interpretation back to its source" (English summary, final paragraph)

**Labels:** useful / keep
**Why:** An editable overview, correctable clusters, traceable interpretations,
visibly provisional suggestions and confirmation before a task counts as agreed
are the five things the group names, and four of the five exist in the client
today, which is the strongest link available between Week 3 and the demo.
