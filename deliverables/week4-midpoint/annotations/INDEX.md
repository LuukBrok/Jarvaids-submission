## Where the material is, and how it was read

The Week 3 material is on the `main` branch, in a folder called
`week 3 materials/`. It was read there with `git show` and **was not copied into
this branch**, because the repository rule is that raw recordings and
transcripts carrying identifiers never enter the public repository, and because
a decision about that folder is still open with the coordinator.

Files marked "not read" carry identifiers, so they were not opened. They are
listed here and in `GAPS.md` as present on main, not read for this annotation,
to be submitted from `evidence/raw/` by the group. **The interviews are
therefore annotated through the MapIt report and the group's own summaries, not
through the transcripts.**

## What the annotations point to for the proposal

1. During the meeting, not after it, is the one claim the material states plainly, and it is the whole differentiator (`flipchart.md` C1).
2. The tool makes the first clusters and the group corrects them by dragging, during the session: the product's rule came from the group before it came from the build (`frameit.md` F2 and F3).
3. What was said and what the model proposed must be distinguishable on sight; the group is certain of that and has not decided how (`frameit.md` F5, `group-summaries.md` S4).
4. Designers want to check a model's themes themselves, which is independent support for provisional until accepted (`interviews-mapit-report.md` A5).
5. Trust breaks on provenance, and a plausible wrong reading is more dangerous than an obviously broken one, so the stand-in reading must be labelled out loud and every item must carry its source (`interviews-mapit-report.md` A4, `group-summaries.md` S6).
6. A suggestion that does not point at a specific note will be ignored, because generic feedback was found useless by interviewees and by the group itself (`interviews-mapit-report.md` A2, `group-summaries.md` S1).
7. Wrong grouping is the failure named first, ahead of misread words, so clustering should be the more cautious of the two (`frameit.md` F4).
8. Three questions stay open and should be presented as open: when suggestions appear, what wins when general advice contradicts an earlier project decision, and how an agreed task differs from one merely mentioned (`frameit.md` F6 and F8).
9. Two things are rejected here: the multi-agent split, chosen before anything was measured, and the 3D tool learning thread, whose source is not in the folder (`flipchart.md` C7, `group-summaries.md` S10).
10. One warning for slide 3: the interview findings reach this proposal through a report the group itself found flattening, so the slide should say the interviews are cited through that report, not quoted from the room (`interviews-mapit-report.md` A8, `group-summaries.md` S3).
