Introduction

Project background

Design teams in working sessions produce remarks, sketches and sticky notes. Most of it is not recorded. Three problems recur.

  1. 1A remark made earlier in the session cannot be found again.
  2. 2The wall is photographed at the end, but the notes are rarely typed up.
  3. 3The board survives the meeting; the reasoning behind it does not.

Source: concept 001, ideation folder

Introduction

Users and context

Designers, technical participants in multidisciplinary teams, and Master’s students in design programmes, in meetings and brainstorm sessions that combine spoken discussion, sketches, sticky notes and photographs. Two to eight people, one to two hours, one facilitator, a shared screen.

Roles: flowchart F4.3; session size: app/server/README.md

Introduction

Product concept

An AI tool used during design meetings and brainstorm sessions. It takes the spoken discussion and photographs of the room as input, and keeps a structured visual overview of the meeting on the digital whiteboard while the meeting is running. Participants can ask it questions and give it commands by voice.

Introduction

Voice commands

A participant says “add this to the planning”. The command interpreter decides whether the sentence was addressed to the tool, works out what “this” refers to from the canvas and the last minute of speech, and proposes one change to the whiteboard. A participant confirms it. When the reference is ambiguous the tool asks rather than guesses.

Problem framing

Design question

How can what is said and written during a design session be recorded as it happens, in a way that keeps the team’s own work distinct from what a model proposed?

Derived from the concept, the week 3 findings and the flowchart

Design principle

Provisional output

Anything produced by a model is marked provisional until a participant accepts it. A provisional note has a dashed edge and names its source. Moving a note does not count as accepting it.

Implementation

From photograph to notes

A participant marks each note on the photograph. A placeholder adapter returns a reading; no handwriting recognition has been run. A participant corrects, accepts or rejects each reading.

Captures of the client. Proposed automatic segmentation and reading: documentation/stack.md
