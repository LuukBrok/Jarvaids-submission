# Automatic workspace

Luuk requested and authorised this behaviour on 20 October after testing the
integrated release. This supersedes the earlier requirement to accept every
piece of input. Capture agreement remains separate.

- The PC launcher enables automatic application. Typed notes and photo readings
  become editable canvas items immediately, with source references and an
  automatic marker. No human acceptance is recorded for these operations.
- Choosing a wall photograph starts detection and transcription in the
  background. Each detected note has a validated normalised crop, text and
  provenance. Manual region selection remains available to legacy clients.
  Whole-photo mode sends the sanitised photograph to the configured reader.
- A usable poll definition opens automatically. Closing voting stops new votes
  and displays the tally without an additional acceptance step. Unidentified
  or ambiguous replies remain visible exceptions, never invented ballots.
- Command lists collect and display additions immediately. Closing stops
  collection; it does not force a review ritual or label suggestions as agreed.
- Every recognised command has ten documented, tested example phrases.
  Lifecycle controls accept polite variants and the observed close-voting ASR
  errors, while the original transcript stays unchanged.
- Results show the useful content first. Editing, evidence and processing
  details are collapsed. Cards grow naturally with content; the canvas pans
  instead of scrolling inside notes.

Costs: automatic OCR can add mistakes without prior review; editing and undo,
source crops and model labels therefore stay available. Whole-wall photographs
include their visible background and can cost more than selected crops. Real
handwriting and microphone accuracy still require a room trial. WP1 and the
summary-to-session bridge remain outside this change.
