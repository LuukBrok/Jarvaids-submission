Spacious UI · exploration 01 of 05

Air

Thesis. Before deciding that an opacity aesthetic is needed, find out how much of it the shipped system already gives you for free. This screen is the live canvas built from the existing tokens with nothing added: no alpha on a colour, no material, no new size, no new radius, no colour that is not already in tokens.css. The only thing that changed is distance. The shipped exemplar of this screen draws thirty-nine 1px edges; this one draws five, and only one of the five is a separator, the session bar. The grouping the others were doing is redrawn in white space: items inside a zone sit 12px apart, zones sit 56px apart, and past about a 1:3 ratio the eye stops reading a list and starts reading groups whether you draw the line or not. The argument is that "spacious, strong hierarchy, gestalt" is mostly a spacing brief, not a material brief, and that the honest baseline for the other four explorations is this one rather than the cramped exemplar. What it cannot do is the other half of the reference: at 15px, the canvas is not readable from four metres, and no amount of air fixes that.

What this costs and what it buys

Design system rules broken

None. Every colour, size, radius and line width on this page is a shipped token used for its stated job. That is the point of having a control condition: if the team prefers one of the other four, they are paying for the difference between that one and this one, not for the difference between it and the exemplar.

What the spacing is actually doing

  • Common region without a region. Fifty-six pixels between zones against twelve inside one. Nothing is drawn. Cover the zone labels and the three groups still separate.
  • Continuity in the rail. The transcript rows have no rule between them at all. The 76px timestamp column aligns every row on one left edge and that edge is the separator.
  • One big type drop. 32px straight to 13px in the page head, with nothing at 22 or 15 in between. A gradual ramp reads as a list of headings; one cliff reads as a title.
  • Thirty-nine drawn edges became five. Counted in Chromium, on the same screen: the shipped exemplar paints 39 one-pixel borders in --color-hairline or --color-hairline-strong, this version paints 5. Four of those five are the four sides of one secondary button, which 1.4.11 requires. The fifth is the session bar, because that boundary is real: above it is the product, below it is the team's work.

Honest limits

This screen is a laptop screen. The product's brief says the desktop case is the room screen, glanced at from across a table, and at 15px body and 12px kind labels that does not work at four metres. Air cannot fix it without leaving the seven-size scale, which is exactly the trade explorations 03 and 04 take on. Air also gains nothing from translucency, because there is nothing behind anything: on a white canvas with no layering, alpha is decoration. That finding is worth as much as the screen.
