Jarvaids design system, colour scheme

One hue, in tonal stepsDelft, the one scheme

A single blue accent over a slightly cool ground, with six status meanings beside it. The provisional mark is the accent itself, so the distinction between what the model proposed and what the team agreed is carried by form and by words, never by hue. This page is for looking rather than reading: the glyph strips are at the size they are drawn, and once in greyscale, because a number cannot tell you whether two 16px shapes are the same shape. Nothing here animates; nothing in the system does. The reasoning is in spec/colour-schemes.md and every ratio is in spec/accessibility.md. Opens offline.

What it costs

A scheme that cannot state its costs is not worth shipping.

Colour no longer separates a proposal from an action. The dash, the glyph and the word do all the work. They were always meant to be the primary signals; now they are the only ones.

Waiting is an ochre close in hue to the failure red. It measures clear of it, and leans on the half-filled ring and the word.

Blue desaturates on a cool projector. Untested in a real room, like every other contrast claim in this repository.

Adding a second scheme is a design decision, not a preference. The mechanism is still in tokens.css; spec/colour-schemes.md has the six steps and the argument a second hue would have to win.
