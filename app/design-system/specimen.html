The canvasWhat the team agreed, and what the model proposed

Accepted: a tonal fill and no line. Proposed: no fill, a 1px dashed accent line and the word. A person's typed note: a solid line, no fill, "Not yet reviewed". Never a fill and a line on one element. The text is never greyed, because an unreviewed item still has to be readable to be reviewed. The board is the page's own ground, with no frame; a group is a sunken panel. A note's paper colour is the small swatch in its meta line, never its fill.

Accepting fades the dashed line out and the tonal fill in over 250ms, the longest transition in the system, because it is the moment the product is about. The selected proposal shows how the accent composes with itself: a 1px dashed line inside, a 2px solid ring outside, the same hue told apart by weight, style and the gap between them. Inside the group, the open question is visible: an accepted note there would share the panel's tone.

Buttons and fields

Four variants, one primary per view. 32px at a desk, 44px in room mode and on touch. A button is a fill or nothing, never a fill and a line. A field is the one place a line marks an edge, because it is a place you type into.
